DEMO COPY · AWAITING FINAL TEXT

Nobody summons a hero for free. The Grail advances the legend, the war spends it, and the difference is collected from whoever is still standing when the noise stops.

This is a story about that arithmetic. About a man who read the terms, understood them completely, and decided the only honest move left was to default on the whole arrangement — heroes, masters, world and all.

He is not a chosen one. He has no crest, no lineage, no inheritance waiting in a vault. He has a debt he did not agree to and a very short window in which to refuse it.

The war does not hate him. That is the worst part. It simply audits him, line by line, and waits for the numbers to balance.

04 / THE EXCERPT

Read a piece.

“Nothing here is a gift. Everything here is an advance.”

The circle did not flare. It itemised. Light came up from the floor in thin, even columns, like a receipt printing itself in the air, and each column had a name at the top of it that I did not recognise and could not stop reading.

When it finished, two of them were standing in the space where the light had been. Neither looked at me first. They looked at each other, the way colleagues do when a job comes through that neither of them wanted.

“You called,” the taller one said.

“I did.”

“Do you know the rate?”

I said nothing. He nodded, as if that answered it, and stepped out of the circle onto the concrete like a man clocking in.

“Then we start,” he said. “And you will find out what you owe at the end, same as everyone.”
