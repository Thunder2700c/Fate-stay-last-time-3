Privacy

EFFECTIVE 26 NOV 2026

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

Your reading place never leaves your browser. We use localStorage only:

  • fslt.player.v1 — last track played and per-chapter progress.
  • fslt.consent — your analytics choice.
  • Old keys from the previous site design are migrated once, locally.

Clearing your browser data erases all of it. No account, no login, no server-side profile.

ANALYTICS

If you press "Allow analytics", Vercel Web Analytics counts page views. It is cookieless and does not fingerprint you. If you decline, nothing loads. There are no ads and no third-party trackers.

THIRD PARTIES

  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (GSAP) serve fonts and animation code — they see standard request data like your IP.
  • Letters you send open as public GitHub issues under your chosen pen name. Don't put anything private in a letter.
  • Hosting is Vercel; standard server logs apply.
