Contact the project.

This page explains the available contact route for the current edition. It does not pretend that a form has been delivered to an inbox.

CONTACT CHANNEL Private inbox not configured

No public email address or secure form endpoint has been configured for this project. Do not enter personal or sensitive information into an unconnected form.

Where to go next

The project repository is the public place to review the website source and its published changes. Public issue reports may be visible to anyone who can access the repository.

Open project repository ↗

Before sending a report

  • Describe the page address where the problem occurred.
  • Explain what you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • Include browser and device details only when they help reproduce the issue.
  • Remove passwords, private account details, phone numbers, and other sensitive information.
Is the public repository a private contact inbox?

No. Public repository activity may be visible to other visitors. Do not post passwords, personal identifiers, private correspondence, or other sensitive information there.

Can I submit a manuscript or chapter here?

No. This website has no manuscript upload, chapter submission, or file-attachment endpoint. The rewritten novel content remains under the author's control.

A clear boundary

This static page does not collect messages, upload attachments, subscribe visitors, or send email. A confirmation page is not evidence that a message was submitted.
