LINER NOTES

Pressed by hand.

This is a fan novel about a broken shoe at a Ludhiana wedding, a video a billion people laughed at, and the man who decided that a world which rewards cruelty with currency is a timeline worth pruning. Gurtej — a Master without Command Seals, a mediator turned warlord, the one the internet named Vardilord — summons Saber and Archer not to win a Grail, but to end the argument.

The deeper the war goes, the thinner the line between righteous fury and wounded ego. That line is the actual story. Ten tracks are pressed. Side E is still being cut.
