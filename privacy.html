What stays on your device

Your place in the story never leaves your browser. We use local storage only:

  • fslt.consent — your analytics choice.
  • Reading position, once chapters arrive, so you can resume where you stopped.

Clearing your browser data erases all of it. No account, no login, no server-side profile.

Analytics

If you press “Allow”, Vercel Web Analytics counts page views. It is cookieless and does not fingerprint you. If you decline, nothing loads. There are no ads and no third-party trackers.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (GSAP) serve fonts and animation code; they see standard request data such as your IP.
  • Letters you send open as public GitHub issues. Keep private matters out of them.
  • Hosting is Vercel; standard server logs apply.
