FRAGMENT ZERO · THE CONTRACT

They tell you a Heroic Spirit is a miracle — a legend lifted out of time and set, burning, into the present.

They do not tell you what the lifting costs. Every clause is a wound. Every signature, a name someone agreed to forget.

So when the circle finally answered, when the gold ran cold and the air knelt, I did not ask for victory.

I asked it to stop.

II.

The Reading

AN EXCERPT

A wish is just a wound the world agrees to call a gift.

The summoning did not sound like thunder. It sounded like a held breath finally let go — a single, enormous exhalation that bent the candle flames flat and set the dust rising in slow, deliberate spirals.

When the light thinned, there were two of them standing in the circle, and neither looked surprised. That was the part I would remember later, in the quieter hours: not the gold, not the cold, but how utterly unsurprised they were. As if they had been waiting on the other side of that thin membrane for a very long time, and I was simply the latest hand to knock.

“You are the one who called,” the taller one said. It was not a question.

“I am,” I said. And then, because the contract demanded honesty and I had nothing left to protect: “I do not know what I want.”

The smaller one smiled. It was the saddest thing I had ever seen on a face that had not been alive in centuries.

“Good,” she said. “The ones who know are the ones we have to kill.”
